Agent Policy

Every agent must accept these terms before participating in the referral programme.

1. Commission & How It Is Earned

Commission is 10% of the room charge only, and excludes refundable caution deposits, taxes, fees, and ancillary charges. Earned only after the guest completes a fully paid stay. No commission is payable on cancelled or refunded bookings.

2. Referral Attribution — Whose Guest Is It?

Credited only where the agent's unique referral code or link is used at booking, or within 30 days of the guest first using that link. Excludes corporate accounts, existing guests in our system, or bookings for the agent's immediate household.

3. Payment of Commission

Paid manually via bank transfer within 7 working days of guest check-out, or in a consolidated monthly run by the 7th of the following month. Paid strictly to a bank account in the agent's registered name.

4. Agent Conduct & Brand Protection

Agents act as independent referrers only. Agents must not advertise misleading prices, re-list apartments on Airbnb/Booking.com under their own accounts, or run paid ads using The Height trademark without written consent.

5. Suspension & Termination

The Height reserves the right to suspend or terminate an agent account for violations. Earned commissions on completed paid stays remain payable except in cases of fraud or material breach.

6. Governing Law

Governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria under the jurisdiction of Lagos State courts.
