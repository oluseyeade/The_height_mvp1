Cancellation Schedule

The Height Apartment operates a standard Moderate Cancellation Policy for all direct and corporate bookings.

Up to 5 Days Before Check-In (Full Refund)

Guests who cancel their reservation at least 5 full days (120 hours) prior to check-in time (2:00 PM on check-in date) will receive a 100% full refund of all payments minus bank transaction processing fees.

Within 5 Days of Check-In (First Night Retained)

If a cancellation occurs less than 5 days before check-in, the payment for the first night's stay is non-refundable. The remaining nights will be refunded in full.

No-Show or Early Departure

In cases of a guest no-show on the check-in date without notice, or early departure after check-in, unused nights are non-refundable.

Corporate & Group Booking Adjustments

Corporate clients with retainer agreements or extended-stay bookings may have custom modification clauses outlined in their signed corporate quote document. Contact our Corporate Desk at 0904 513 5890 for assistance.
