House Rules

To ensure comfort, safety, and a premium experience for all guests, please review our house rules.

The Height Premium Standards

Property Conduct Policy

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in is from 2:00 PM onwards. Check-out is strictly by 12:00 PM (Noon). Late check-out without prior confirmation may incur additional charges.

No Smoking Inside Suites

Smoking inside the suites or bedrooms is strictly prohibited. Designated outdoor balcony or garden areas are provided for smoking.

Quiet Hours & Parties

Quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM. Loud music, disruptive parties, and unauthorized gatherings are strictly forbidden.

Guest Capacity Limits

Only registered guests declared at booking are permitted for overnight stays. Visitors must vacate by 10:00 PM and register at the security gate.

Security & Identification

All adult guests must present a valid government-issued ID (Passport, Driver's License, NIN) at check-in for NDPR security compliance.

Property & Amenities

Please treat all furniture, appliances, and fixtures with care. Any damage, loss, or missing items will be charged to the guest account.
